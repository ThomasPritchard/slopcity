# Original Slop City assets

Authored in Blender 5.2.1 LTS through the included Python scripts. These meshes, materials, rig, animations, HDR lighting and ripple normals were created for this project; no downloaded character or environment asset pack is used.

The character direction is deliberately endearing: the original fixed, wide-eyed stare and a neutral mouth. Keep that identity while improving clothing and environmental detail.

- `citizen.blend`: rigged citizen, modular jackets/knitwear, trousers, sneakers/boots/loafers; Idle, Walk, Wave, Sit, Run, Jump, HandshakeA/B and HugA/B clips. `clothing.py` supplies the additional garments. `add_citizen_interactions.py`, called by `build_assets.py`, authors the six additional clips on the existing rig without root motion; runtime owns movement and the shared timeline.
- `street-kit.blend`: original street furniture assembly. The dedicated sources below are current for the benches, fountain, lamp posts and planting; `build_environment.py` delegates those models to their dedicated builders.
- `town-ground.blend`: patterned limestone promenades, concentric fountain paving, framed bench landings and compacted gravel courts. `build_ground.py` owns only this source and `town-ground.glb`; all stone/gravel colour, normal and roughness maps are generated and packed into both.
- `bench.blend`: editable civic bench with six rounded oak seat slats, a gently reclined five-slat back, timber arm caps, swept evergreen cast supports, stretcher, mounting shoes and bronze fasteners. `build_bench.py` owns only this source and `bench.glb`; its original timber grain is packed into both files.
- `fountain.blend`: The Rising Wave, an original twisting bronze sculpture on a limestone plinth, a segmented stone basin, glazed mosaic floor and eight exposed jet nozzles. `build_fountain.py` owns only this source and `fountain.glb`; dynamic water is authored separately in `src/world/fountain.ts`.
- `tree.blend`, `planter.blend`, `entrance-planter.blend`: original full-crowned branching civic trees and two sizes of hollow limestone planting bed, with recessed loam, mulch, folded grass and seed heads. `build_planting.py` owns only these three source/export pairs; foliage UVs preserve root-to-tip wind weights and clump phases.
- `lamp.blend`: editable civic lantern post with a cast-metal base and tapered shaft, bronze collars, six glazed panels, an opal chimney, vented roof and finial. `build_lamp.py` owns only this source and `lamp.glb`; its `lamp-light-source` marker anchors the browser point light.
- `signpost.blend`: editable cast-metal town-square sign with a pedestal, rolled panel rim, rear clamps and readable enamel lettering on both sides. `build_signpost.py` owns only this source and `signpost.glb`; it uses Blender's bundled Bfont to render and pack the original lettering, with no external fonts or texture dependencies.
- `changing-room.blend`: room, timber floor/slats, mirror, lighting strips, curtains, rail, stool and platform.
- `casino-sign.blend` / `casino-entry-sign.blend` / `casino-tagline.blend`: matching enamel fascia, double-sided hanging entrance plaque and interior slogan for The Meridian. `build_casino_signage.py` writes only these three source/export pairs through `signage.py`; it does not regenerate the casino kit or roulette wheel.
- `shop-sign.blend` / `shop-tagline.blend`: matching wall-mounted enamel shop fascia and interior plaque, with rolled bronze edges, face screws and wall standoffs. `build_shop_signage.py` uses the shared `signage.py` plaque builder and signpost lettering renderer, and writes only these two source/export pairs.
- `form-thread-shell.blend`, `form-thread-roof.blend`, `clothing-shop.blend`: the 20 × 16 m boutique, with display glazing, striped awnings, a complete ceiling, connected departments, parquet, six clothing rails, checkout, stock displays and two fitting bays. `build_shop.py` owns these three maintained source/export pairs; it reads `shared/shop-layout.json` and does not import the disposable design-study sources.
- `meridian-shell.blend`: expanded limestone venue, square-level foyer, five stairs, east ramp, rounded red-neon marquee, warm bulb soffit and illuminated glassblock entrance piers.
- `meridian-roof.blend`: exterior weather envelope, closed upper facade and weather coping; interior ceilings are a separate asset.
- `meridian-ceiling.blend`: fixed ivory/bronze Art Deco coffers, recessed warm cove lines, perimeter cornices and a central sunburst medallion.
- `meridian-chandelier.blend`: grand four-tier bronze chandelier with opal ribbons, faceted crystal drops and a central finial, authored at town `(x, z) = (0, 38)`.
- `meridian-interior.blend`: right reception facing inward, left inactive ATM placeholder, enclosed foyer/stair walls, a grand stair portal, framed ramp doorway, remaining traversal rails, wall lanterns and floor inlays. Game furnishings are separate instances.
- `roulette-table.blend`, `blackjack-table.blend`, `slot-machine.blend`, `casino-chair.blend`: reusable floor-origin walnut/bronze/evergreen furniture with shaped profiles, curved upholstery, readable table markings and a blank slot display opening for live browser reels.
- `roulette-wheel.blend`: separate numbered 37-pocket rotor, spindle and dividers for runtime animation, rebuilt with the modular Meridian furniture.
- `craps-table.blend`: walnut and bronze standing table with padded evergreen rails, rebound cushions, two playable line fields and six point positions. `build_craps.py` owns its source/export independently of the casino kit.
- `casino-kit.blend`: retained legacy casino assembly; the expanded runtime and `build_casino.py` use the modular sources above.
- `build_citizen_lod.py`: derives `citizen-lod.glb` from the unchanged citizen source; `citizen-lod.stats.json` records mesh/rig/animation checks and triangle reduction.
- `../public/models/*.glb`: runtime exports.
- `../public/textures/`: original generated lighting environment and water normal map.
